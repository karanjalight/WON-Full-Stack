## 🎨 Image Quality Guidelines

### DO:
✅ Use high-resolution images (minimum 1920px width for hero)
✅ Choose diverse, inclusive representations
✅ Select images with good lighting and composition
✅ Ensure images align with WON's professional brand
✅ Optimize for web (compress without losing quality)
✅ Use consistent color tones across sections

### DON'T:
❌ Use watermarked or copyrighted images
❌ Mix drastically different photography styles
❌ Use overly staged or stock-photo-looking images
❌ Ignore mobile responsiveness
❌ Upload unoptimized, large file sizes

## 🌍 Cultural Authenticity

Since WON is "Africa's gateway to global academic excellence," prioritize:

1. **African Student Representation**: 
   - At least 50% of student images should feature African students
   - Show diverse African countries/backgrounds

2. **Global Context**:
   - Mix of African and international students
   - International competition venues
   - Cultural diversity in tutors and participants

3. **Aspirational Yet Authentic**:
   - Real study environments
   - Genuine academic settings
   - Avoid overly polished/corporate imagery
