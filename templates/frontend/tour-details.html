International Mathematics Olympiad (IMO) 2025

The International Mathematics Olympiad (IMO) is the world's most prestigious mathematics competition for high school students. Since 1959, the IMO has brought together the brightest young mathematical minds from over 100 countries to solve challenging problems that test creativity, perseverance, and mathematical insight beyond the standard curriculum.

This year's competition features six challenging problems designed to test problem-solving abilities, mathematical creativity, and logical reasoning. Participants will have two consecutive days to solve three problems each day, with 4.5 hours allocated per session. The competition covers areas including algebra, combinatorics, geometry, and number theory.

Join thousands of talented students worldwide in this intellectually stimulating competition. Whether you're aiming for a medal or simply want to challenge yourself with world-class mathematics problems, the IMO provides an unparalleled opportunity to showcase your mathematical talent and connect with like-minded peers from around the globe.

Competition Schedule

Welcome to IMO 2025! Check-in begins at 9:00 AM. Participants will receive their competition materials, ID badges, and information packets. The opening ceremony starts at 2:00 PM, featuring keynote speakers and an introduction to the competition rules and format. Networking session follows at 4:00 PM.
Competition Day 1: Participants will tackle problems 1, 2, and 3. The exam session runs from 9:00 AM to 1:30 PM (4.5 hours). Each problem is worth 7 points. Topics covered include algebra, number theory, and combinatorics. Lunch break at 2:00 PM, followed by optional problem discussion sessions at 4:00 PM.
Competition Day 2: Participants will solve problems 4, 5, and 6. The exam session runs from 9:00 AM to 1:30 PM (4.5 hours). Topics include geometry, functional equations, and advanced problem-solving. These problems are typically more challenging than Day 1. Evening relaxation activities and cultural exchange programs available.
Final day featuring the awards ceremony at 3:00 PM. Gold, Silver, and Bronze medals will be awarded to top performers. All participants receive official IMO certificates. Special recognition for perfect scores and outstanding solutions. Closing remarks by IMO leadership and announcement of next year's host country. Networking reception at 5:00 PM.
