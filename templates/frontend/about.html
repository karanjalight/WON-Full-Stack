Why WON

Africa's gateway to global academic excellence

WON (World Olympiad Network) helps students and schools discover Olympiads, apply confidently, and access preparation, mentorship, and travel support.

  • Verified Olympiad listings and guidance
  • Preparation resources and expert tutors
  • Application, logistics, and travel support

A single platform for Olympiad discovery, preparation, and participation

We partner with trusted Olympiad organizers and educators to help students build confidence, compete globally, and showcase their talents.

From applications to travel arrangements, WON makes the journey clear and achievable for learners and families.
